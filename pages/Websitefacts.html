What is Food For All?

Our Mission:

At Food For All our primary goal is to distribute many meals across the world to hungry, impoverished, and less fortunate individuals. We started this company as a means to spread good in the world and we hope that it will continue to grow as more users learn about it.

Fun Facts!

As of right now our team has distributed 23,486 meals nationwide.
We have distributed to 17 different countries total. Our team only consists of 3 people!! We are a nonprofit organization and do everything for free.
